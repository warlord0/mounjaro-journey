Another Sunday dinner produced an even more ridiculous example.

I had to resist eating **two roast potatoes**.

Two.

They were right there.

They looked fantastic.

I wanted them.

But I was full.

So I didn't eat them.

This isn't some heroic tale of discipline.

It's two fucking roast potatoes.

But for me, the interesting thing is that I actually had to make the decision.

I had to recognise:

> *I want these.*

and then:

> *I don't need these.*

That's a new distinction for me.

Fortunately, the potatoes weren't wasted.

We have two Rottweilers.

**My loss is their win.**

Sunday is their official leftover acquisition day.
