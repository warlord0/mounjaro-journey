There's another way I've started to notice the weight I've lost.

I cycle to work, and I carry a backpack with me.

It has my laptop, a change of clothes and all the various bits and pieces I need during the day. It's not particularly light.

One day it occurred to me that I've now lost **more weight from my body than the weight of the backpack I'm carrying**.

And I can feel it.

I know what that backpack feels like when I'm cycling. I know what it feels like when I take it off.

I've effectively taken even more weight than that off myself permanently.

That's a much more visceral way of understanding the change than looking at a number on the bathroom scales.

The scales say I've lost around 11 kg.

My brain says:

> *That's a number.*

My backpack says:

> *No, mate. You have lost **this much stuff**.*

And when I'm cycling up a hill, I can actually feel the difference.

It's one of those little things that makes the weight loss suddenly seem real.
