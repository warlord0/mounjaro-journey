**14 st 11¼ lb.**

That's **207.25 lb**, or **94.0 kg**.

From a starting point of **16 st 10 lb (106.1 kg)**, that's:

* **26.75 lb (12.1 kg) lost**
* About **1 st 12¾ lb** gone
* Roughly **11.4%** of my starting weight

My BMI is now around **29.7**.

I've officially crossed below BMI 30.

---

The number on its own doesn't mean a great deal to me. What means something is what it translates to.

I'm now carrying around **12 kg less person** than when I started.

That's more than my actual work backpack weighs. Laptop, change of clothes, all of it.

No target weight required for that to matter. I'm not chasing 90 kg, or 85 kg, or whatever figure a calculator happens to like the look of. I'm just getting closer to the thing I actually wanted in the first place: a body that's easier to move around in.
