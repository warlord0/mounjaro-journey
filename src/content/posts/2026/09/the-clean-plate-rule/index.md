I'm still only on 2.5mg. I keep half-wondering whether I should be pushing the dose up, and I had to properly think through why I haven't.

Higher doses aren't some separate mechanism. They don't burn more calories on their own. What they do is turn the volume down further on wanting to eat, which makes it easier to hold a deficit. That's still a deficit I have to actually maintain. The dose doesn't do that part for me. It just makes it less of a fight.

So the real question was never "do I need a bigger dose." It's what I'm doing with the appetite I've already got quietened down.

---

My actual pattern, on an average day, is: breakfast, cornflakes or toast, then more or less nothing until dinner. Dinner isn't bigger than it used to be. It's exactly the same size it's always been.

And I clean the plate. Every time.

Not because I'm starving by then. Because it's there, I like food, and I hate waste. Put those three things together and the plate gets finished, whether or not I actually needed the last few mouthfuls.

---

That's the bit I actually have to work on. Not portion size, not skipping meals, not counting anything. The instinct that says food on a plate gets eaten, regardless of whether I still want it by the time I get there.

So the experiment is deliberately simple: serve dinner exactly as normal, and stop treating an empty plate as the goal. If there's a bit left, leave it.

I already know I can do this. I've done it with the KitKat on the desk, with the doughnut, with actual roast potatoes on a Sunday plate. This is just applying the same decision somewhere it hasn't really been tested yet — at the end of an ordinary dinner, with nothing dramatic about it, just food that's still there when I'm done wanting it.

I expect the first few times to feel properly wrong. Not because I'm still hungry. Because a lifetime of "there's food left, so eat it" doesn't switch off just because I've decided it should.

That's the difference between physical hunger and mental hunger again, just showing up somewhere new. Mounjaro's made the first one quieter. The second one is still entirely mine to sort out, one unfinished plate at a time.
