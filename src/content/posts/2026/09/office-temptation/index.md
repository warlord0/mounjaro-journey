It's not very often the office kitchen has treats in it. When it does, it's grossly unfair.

And this time it's unfair in a repetitive way.

---

These aren't today's treats. They've been there a while now, a legacy of someone bringing things back after a holiday, and nobody's cleared them. Either everyone else doesn't like them, or they're all trying to be good too. Whichever it is, the result is the same. Every time I go in, they're still there.

---

There are two of them.

The first is a packet of foreign gummy sweets with liquid centres. I can ignore those completely. No effort involved.

The second is a tub of Turkish cinnamon biscuits, and I actually like those.

The tub has smaller packs inside, five biscuits to a pack. Which is the clever bit, really, and the worst bit. Nobody has to commit to a tub. It's just one small pack of five.

---

What makes it worse is that there's no ending to it.

The doughnuts I wrote about had a yellow label, a lifespan, a point where the argument would be settled one way or the other. These don't. The biscuits just sit there in the kitchen, in a tub full of individual packs, waiting.

An indefinite temptation. Not so much a battle as a permanent feature of the room.

I'll keep going in, and I'll keep walking past. But somebody could really just finish them off. Somebody who isn't me.
