Thunder Belly usually has the decency to wait for Tuesday. This week it turned up on Saturday instead, six in the morning, before I was even out of bed.

I assumed it was a one-off. It wasn't. Sunday, same thing, same time, woke me again. Two days of rumbling, wind, and an uncomfortable stomach, no diarrhoea involved. And the eggy burps were back, a return visit rather than anything new — I'd had those before, just not for a while. And it didn't stop there. Monday, and it's still hanging around.

---

Four or five months on 2.5 mg, and I thought I'd learned the pattern. Apparently the pattern doesn't care that I'd learned it.

It's not unmanageable, just frustrating. Nothing like the 5 mg disaster. Just my digestive system deciding to misbehave early and overstay its welcome, well past the weekend and into the working week.

---

Oddly, there's a bit of me that's almost pleased about it. It's a reminder that 2.5 mg is still doing something down there, not just coasting along doing nothing. Not that I needed convincing — the scales have already told me that story, 14 st 2 lb and still moving. But it's one more data point that the dose hasn't gone quiet on me.
