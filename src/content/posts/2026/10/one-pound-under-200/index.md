14 st 3 lb. Written like that, it barely looks like progress. One pound down from last time, the kind of change that could just be what I had for breakfast.

Write the same number differently, though. 199 lbs. That's not "one pound down." That's the first time in this whole thing I've been under 200.

---

Nothing actually changed between those two numbers. They're the exact same weight. Stone and pounds hid the milestone from me. Pounds on their own handed it straight over. A number starting with a 1 instead of a 2 feels like crossing something, even though the scales didn't do anything dramatic to get me there.

---

I know this isn't the finish line. I know next week's number isn't guaranteed to start with a 1 either. I'm not going to skip celebrating it just because it's small, or because tomorrow might put me back on the other side of it.

Small wins, slow pace. If I only celebrated the big ones, I'd be waiting a very long time between celebrations, and that's not a deal I'm willing to make with myself.

199 it is. For today, anyway.
