14 st 6.5 lb this morning.

I know it won't still say that next week. Water, food still working its way through, whatever I had for dinner the night before — the number moves for reasons that have nothing to do with fat loss, and I know that well enough by now to say it without needing convincing.

None of that stopped me smiling at it.

It's not a milestone I set out chasing. I never picked a target weight to aim for. It's just a number that happened to be lower than the one before it, on a morning when I happened to see it.

That's apparently enough.
