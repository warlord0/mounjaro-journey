Sunday dinner hit again. Roast pork, crispy crackling, a proper mountain of roast potatoes, green beans and broccoli to make it look vaguely sensible. I've said before that if I die on a Monday, at least I won't have missed one of these.

Probably not the finest choice for weight loss. I don't care.

---

This isn't the same as mindlessly working through a packet of something because it's sitting there. It's a meal I genuinely look forward to all week, cooked by the wife, eaten slowly and properly enjoyed. Mounjaro's taken a lot of the mindless eating out of my life. It hasn't touched this one, and I wouldn't want it to.

I'd honestly forgo every other meal in the week just to keep this one. Water included, if it came to it, though I'm told that's not negotiable.

---

One casualty: Rosie and Dexter were supposed to get a share of the pork. They didn't. I knew there was pork, I knew what they were expecting, and I ate the evidence anyway. Next Sunday I'll try putting their bit aside before I start on mine, though I'm not promising anything once the crackling's in front of me.
