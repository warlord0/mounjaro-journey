Ran a day late with the jab this week. Normally it's Tuesday, this time it didn't happen until Wednesday. And right on cue, Wednesday started behaving like it was the day after injection day — stomach churning, gurgling away, the usual.

---

Interesting more than annoying, this one. It's the kind of thing that confirms what I already suspected rather than tells me anything new: the gut reaction isn't tied to the day of the week, it's tied to the jab itself. Push the jab back a day and the stomach drama slides back a day with it, like clockwork.

---

Small bit of reassurance in that, oddly. Not that I needed more proof Mounjaro's doing something, but it's nice when the evidence lines up this cleanly.
