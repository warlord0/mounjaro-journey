Thursday I was already craving a bottle of red. Friday's normally a rum or Jack day, but the Merlot was on offer, so Merlot it was.

---

It was a nice change. It was also gone a lot quicker than the rum ever is. Rum and Pepsi Max has a natural stopping point — 35 ml, maybe a second one, and that's that. A bottle of red doesn't work like that. The glass gets topped up, the bottle stays open, and somehow the evening just keeps going.

---

Here's the bit that actually matters, though. It wasn't wine instead of rum. It was wine, and then rum anyway. The Merlot turned out to be an appetiser, not a replacement, which meant I'd quietly added a second drinking session to the evening rather than swapped one thing for another.

So Friday wine can't become a thing. Not because I didn't enjoy it — I did — but because it doesn't stay in its own lane. Back to pirate petrol next week. Merlot's failed its job interview.
