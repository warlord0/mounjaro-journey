Not Tuesday morning, but it arrived like it was. Stomach gurgling for a couple of hours, eggy burps on and off all day, then the wind really got going, and eventually the full explosive finish. Twelve hours early, give or take.

Small mercy: it waited until the evening. Last time this got going properly it would have been fine sitting in an office meeting. Tonight it just had to contend with the living room.

---

Manageable so far. I've still got the electrolyte sachets left over from the 5 mg disaster, so those are getting used properly this time — small regular sips rather than waiting until I feel rough. Given my kidneys aren't at their best, I'm not taking chances with fluid loss just because the diarrhoea itself feels manageable.

---

Calling this one an incident rather than an episode. Isolated, contained, no lasting damage. The 5 mg version was a proper episode — sustained, miserable, days of it. This was unpleasant for an evening and then done. I'll take an early, contained incident over a late, drawn-out one any day of the week, Tuesday or otherwise.

---

Is this even unusual, though? Five months in, still on the low 2.5 mg dose, and my body's still clearly aware something's going on. Part of me expected it to have settled into quiet background noise by now, not still putting on the occasional full performance. But I suppose it's the same answer as last time — it's evidence the dose is still doing something, even if the something is occasionally inconvenient rather than purely helpful.
