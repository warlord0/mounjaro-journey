Start of celebration season, as far as I'm concerned. We ended up out for lunch yesterday, completely unplanned — turned out our sons were off work, and so were we, so a pub lunch happened instead of whatever we were meant to be doing. I'd already had a bacon batch for breakfast, then a three-bean chilli with half-and-half chips and rice, and a pint of Guinness. Honestly, I could have happily stayed for the afternoon and kept drinking.

---

By evening I genuinely wasn't hungry. Not even slightly. But the wife had leftover Popeyes chicken going, and I knew I'd normally eat something in the evening, so I stuck two pieces in the air fryer with a bit of hot sauce anyway.

Not hunger. Just habit filling in for it, because the evening slot's always had food in it and some part of me still expects it to.

---

Worth noticing, that. The stomach wasn't asking for anything. My head just assumed it should be fed regardless. If celebration season's starting this early, I suspect that particular habit is going to get a proper workout before it's done.
