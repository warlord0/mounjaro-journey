Update on the KitKat that stopped calling my name: it's still on my desk, still uneaten. And it's not just that one. I haven't eaten a single KitKat in weeks.

I won't pretend it's been total abstinence from chocolate — two Border dark chocolate and ginger bars have gone, and happily so. But that's about it. Chocolate's become a genuine rarity for me these days, rather than the thing sitting within arm's reach getting slowly worked through.

The KitKat, meanwhile, just sits there. Uneaten, unbothered, apparently going nowhere.
