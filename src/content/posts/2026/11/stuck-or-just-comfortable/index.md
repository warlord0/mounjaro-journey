Stuck at 14 st 3 lb. Can't seem to push past it, and more Mounjaro isn't the answer — I'm not about to go back up to 5 mg after what that did to me last time.

I'll admit the drinking's crept up lately. We knew this bit was coming. Liquid calories are still calories, as I found out properly when I actually did the maths on it.

So this week, I'm behaving myself. Right up until Friday, which is the wife's birthday weekend — she's away, which for me means drink and takeaways. Then I'm off work until Thursday on annual leave, which is mostly an excuse for Jack and Max to stop over.

So: behaving myself, for about four days, before a fairly comprehensive exception schedule kicks in. Not hypocrisy exactly. Just real life, with all its birthdays and visitors and annual leave still fully intact.

---

What does success actually look like when I never set a target weight? I started this wanting to lose weight and feel better, not to hit a specific number. Fourteen stone three is a genuinely long way from sixteen ten. But without a number to aim at, it's tempting to let the scales redefine success every single week — stall for a fortnight and suddenly it feels like failure, even though nothing about the actual progress has gone backwards.

I don't think the dose has stopped working just because the weight's plateaued. It got me from there to here. What happens next might just be a different kind of work than the first bit was.

---

On balance, though: 14 st 3 lb isn't bad. If you'd handed me that number back at 16 st 10, I'd have bitten your hand off for it. I'm not unhappy with where I am. I'm just not quite ready to put my feet up and call it finished either. No target, no finish line, just a sense that there's probably a bit more progress left in this if I want it.
