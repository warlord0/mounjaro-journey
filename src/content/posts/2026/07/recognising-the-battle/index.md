This is something I think is easy to miss when people talk about weight-loss medication.

It would be tempting to write:

> *Mounjaro stopped me being hungry, so I stopped eating so much and everything became easy.*

That's not my experience.

The medication has changed the physical side of eating.

It has given me a much better chance of recognising when I've had enough.

But the psychological side is still there, and sometimes the effort required to say **no** to something is surprisingly large, even when I know perfectly well that I'm not hungry.

That's probably the hardest part of the whole thing.

I'm not discovering that I have no self-control.

I'm discovering just how much self-control I've been asking myself to use.
