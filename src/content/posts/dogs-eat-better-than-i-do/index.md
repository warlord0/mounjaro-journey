Rosie and Dexter are fed raw food with vegetables.

They happily eat broccoli, carrots and cabbage.

Rosie particularly likes apples.

So there are days when I look at their diet and think they've got this whole nutrition thing rather more organised than I do.

Their main contribution to my weight-loss programme is somewhat less sophisticated.

They eat my Sunday leftovers.

They are particularly enthusiastic about potatoes.

So if I leave two roast potatoes behind, I haven't really lost anything.

I've simply transferred ownership.

**My loss. Their win.**
