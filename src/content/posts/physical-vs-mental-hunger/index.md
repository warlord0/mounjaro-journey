One of the biggest changes I've noticed is that physical hunger is much quieter.

I can go surprisingly long periods without eating.

One day I had cornflakes at around 10am and didn't eat again until about 7:30pm.

Previously, I'd have expected to be absolutely ravenous.

Instead, I simply wasn't particularly hungry.

That is a strange experience.

But there's another side to it.

My brain hasn't stopped wanting things.

I've had evenings where I've finished dinner, my stomach feels completely satisfied, and my brain starts:

> *There's crisps in the kitchen.*

Then:

> *There's chocolate.*

Then:

> *There's a Pepperami.*

It's not hunger.

I know it's not hunger.

That's the problem.

**Knowing doesn't make it easy.**
