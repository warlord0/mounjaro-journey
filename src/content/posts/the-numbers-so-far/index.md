I started at approximately:

**16 st 10 lb / 106 kg**

I've since reached around:

**15 st 1 lb / 95 kg**

That's roughly:

**11 kg / 24 lb lost.**

My BMI is now around **30.1**.

So I'm getting close to dropping below BMI 30.

But I'm deliberately not setting a target weight.

That's not what I'm trying to achieve.

I don't know whether the number that eventually works for me will be 90 kg, 85 kg, 80 kg or something else entirely.

I don't want to spend the next year chasing a number because a calculator says it is theoretically ideal.

I want to find out what weight lets me live my life more easily.
